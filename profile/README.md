# AusConnect Innovation Alliance (ACIA)

AusConnect Innovation Alliance Incorporated (ACIA) is a not-for-profit incorporated association registered in New South Wales.

Through open events, eight professional committees and international collaboration, ACIA helps people and organisations across Australia and the Asia-Pacific learn about, use and shape AI and emerging technology.

## What ACIA does

- Public AI education — talks, workshops, hackathons and training, many of them free
- Inclusion and access — widening who takes part in technology
- Research to practice — bringing universities, researchers and practitioners together
- International cooperation and public policy — AI governance forums and policy consultations
